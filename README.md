### About

Data Platform Engineer with 8+ years across retail, fintech, and fuel/convenience. I build the systems other teams rely on: ingestion, data contracts, lineage, and the internal tooling that makes pipelines cheap to write and safe to run.

- 🧪 Built internal tooling that validates **data contracts** (type, size, nullability, naming conventions) and tracks **lineage** across integrations, replacing weeks of manual spreadsheet review with executable checks. Publication is gated on schema and referential-integrity checks, so a malformed table is held back rather than shipped.
- 🤖 Working on the **data foundations for AI**: evaluation harnesses with quality bars fixed before the results come in, LLM features that ship switched off and are enabled only on evidence, and personal data kept away from external models by the structure of the code rather than by configuration.
- ⚙️ Background leading **event-driven systems at scale**: tech lead of a privacy engineering squad owning 6+ production applications, including a consent orchestration platform answering **6,000+ data subject requests per year** consistently under 4 days against a 15-day legal SLA.
- 🔄 Led an architectural refactor from polling-based ingestion to fully event-driven across 6 downstream systems (parallelism redesign, API-based consumption).
- 🌎 Remote-first, based in Brazil (UTC-3), working in English with distributed teams.

Happy to talk about data platforms, data contracts and data foundations for AI: **emmanuelhcc@gmail.com**.
